Brief client — Nemo & Nini

Project kickoff · v1.2 · 08 October 2026 · Confidential
1. Context

Nemo & Nini is a desktop companion that lives in the top bar of the screen. It reads what you are working on, asks before it acts, and stays out of the way when you are typing.

Fig. 1 — companion parked in the island, expanded view
2. Deliverables
  • Windows 11 desktop prototype, fully interactive
  • Taskbar, window manager, Start menu and shell flyouts
  • File Explorer with a working Downloads folder
  • Motion spec for the island and both companions
3. Timeline

Hand-off on Friday 3 October. Two review rounds included, after which the source files move to the client repository.

Companion

Nemo and Nini both live in the island. Drag either one out, or click them.

Put the cursor on a companion and click it, then: S sit / stand · P pet · F feed · Z sleep · R send home · 3 clicks = cry. O over the island lets them back out.

Nemo on the desktop

The only character who leaves the island. The cursor comes near, he points and runs to you. Hover to stop him, click to slap, drag to carry — and dropping him from high up hurts.
